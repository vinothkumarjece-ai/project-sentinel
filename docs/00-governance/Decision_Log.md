## PS-DEC-001

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Title:** Product Vision

### Decision

Build an AI-powered Operational Intelligence Platform that augments enterprise engineering teams through intelligent investigation, workflow-aware automation, and enterprise knowledge.

### Why

- Growing enterprise complexity
- Multi-vendor environments
- Shortage of experienced engineers
- Need to reduce MTTR
- Preserve organizational knowledge

### Alternatives Considered

- Network Monitoring Platform
- AIOps Dashboard
- AI Chatbot

### Status

Approved

## PS-DEC-002

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Title:** Project Positioning

### Decision

Project Sentinel will be positioned as an AI-powered Operational Intelligence Platform rather than a traditional network monitoring or observability tool.

### Reason

Enterprise organizations increasingly struggle with operational complexity, multi-vendor environments, and shortages of experienced engineers. Existing monitoring tools provide visibility but often leave investigation, reasoning, and cross-team collaboration as manual processes.

### Expected Benefits

- Reduce investigation effort
- Improve engineer productivity
- Reduce MTTR
- Preserve organizational knowledge
- Complement existing monitoring platforms

### Status

Approved

### Owner

Founder
