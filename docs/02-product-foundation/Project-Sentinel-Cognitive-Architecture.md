# Project Sentinel Cognitive Architecture (PSCA)

## Purpose

The Project Sentinel Cognitive Architecture (PSCA) defines the fundamental intelligence model of Project Sentinel.

Rather than functioning as a collection of independent software modules, Project Sentinel is designed as a cognitive system that continuously observes, understands, reasons, learns and assists enterprise operations.

The Cognitive Architecture establishes how every intelligence engine collaborates to transform enterprise operational evidence into trusted operational intelligence.

---

# Why Cognitive Architecture?

Enterprise operations are dynamic.

Every incident is different.

Every enterprise is different.

Every engineer reasons differently based on available evidence and experience.

Traditional software follows predefined workflows.

Traditional monitoring generates alerts.

Traditional automation executes predefined actions.

Project Sentinel follows a different philosophy.

Sentinel thinks before it recommends.

---

# Vision

Project Sentinel shall become the cognitive layer that sits above enterprise infrastructure.

It shall understand:

- Business
- Technology
- Operations
- People
- Processes
- Knowledge
- History
- Risk

before recommending operational decisions.

---

# Design Principles

The Cognitive Architecture follows eight permanent principles.

## 1. Observe Everything

Collect operational evidence without assumptions.

---

## 2. Understand Before Acting

Never recommend actions without understanding the operational situation.

---

## 3. Preserve Organizational Memory

Knowledge shall remain with the enterprise rather than individual engineers.

---

## 4. Explain Every Decision

Every recommendation shall be supported by observable evidence and explainable reasoning.

---

## 5. Learn Continuously

Every incident strengthens future reasoning.

Operational intelligence continuously evolves.

---

## 6. Human Centric

Sentinel assists engineers.

Operational ownership always remains with authorized personnel.

---

## 7. Enterprise First

Business impact always receives higher priority than technical events.

---

## 8. Trust by Design

Security, transparency and customer ownership remain fundamental architectural principles.

# Cognitive Intelligence Engines

The Project Sentinel Cognitive Architecture is composed of specialized intelligence engines.

Each engine performs one cognitive responsibility while collaborating with every other engine.

Current architecture includes:

- Incident Understanding Engine
- Evidence Intelligence Engine
- Enterprise Memory Fabric
- Operational Reasoning Engine
- Confidence Intelligence Engine
- Recommendation Intelligence Engine
- Learning Intelligence Engine
- Operational Risk Intelligence Engine
- Business Impact Intelligence Engine
- Enterprise Adaptation Engine
- Workflow Intelligence Engine
- Continuous Improvement Engine

Together these engines create one unified cognitive system.

Project Sentinel is not designed to become another monitoring platform.

As organizations evolve, the Cognitive Architecture shall evolve with them while preserving operational trust, explainability and human oversight.
