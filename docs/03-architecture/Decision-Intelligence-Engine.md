# Decision Intelligence Engine (DIE)

## Purpose

The Decision Intelligence Engine (DIE) transforms operational reasoning into actionable enterprise decisions.

While the Operational Reasoning Engine determines the most probable operational situation, the Decision Intelligence Engine determines the most appropriate operational response.

Its purpose is to recommend decisions that are technically sound, operationally practical, business aware and aligned with enterprise policies.

---

# Philosophy

Correct reasoning does not automatically result in the correct operational decision.

Operational decisions depend upon:

- Business impact
- Operational risk
- Enterprise policies
- Current operational state
- Available resources
- Service commitments

The Decision Intelligence Engine evaluates these factors before proposing any action.

---

# Vision

Sentinel shall recommend decisions in the same structured manner as an experienced Operations Manager leading a Major Incident.

---

# Core Objectives

The Decision Intelligence Engine shall:

- Determine the next best operational action.
- Evaluate operational risk.
- Consider business priorities.
- Respect enterprise policies.
- Avoid unnecessary escalations.
- Reduce decision delays.
- Maintain explainability.

Business Aware

Explainable

Auditable

Every proposed decision shall answer:

Why?

Which evidence supports it?

Which policy influenced it?

What risks exist?

What alternatives were considered?
