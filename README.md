# Project Sentinel

## Vision

Project Sentinel is an AI-powered Operational Intelligence Platform designed to augment enterprise engineering teams by reducing investigation effort, accelerating incident resolution, and preserving operational knowledge.

## Mission

Help enterprises reduce operational complexity by combining AI reasoning, workflow awareness, and domain expertise into a single platform.

## Current Status

🟢 Founder Research Phase

## Product Principles

- Assist engineers, don't replace them.
- Reduce Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR).
- Support multi-vendor enterprise environments.
- Capture and preserve operational knowledge.
- Build evidence-based AI reasoning.
